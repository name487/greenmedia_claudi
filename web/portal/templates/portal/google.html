Getting “Error 400: redirect_uri_mismatch” from Google?

Add this exact address under Authorized redirect URIs on the OAuth client in Google Cloud (APIs & Services → Credentials), save, and wait ~5 minutes:

{{ status.redirect_uri }}

It must match character for character — localhost vs 127.0.0.1, http vs https, the port and a trailing slash all count. To change it, set GOOGLE_REDIRECT_URI in backend/.env and restart the API. When Google asks, sign in with the account that manages the Business Profiles.
